Delete your account and data

You can request permanent deletion of your Mediary account and every record associated with it, whether or not you still have the app installed.

Option 1 — delete it yourself in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Mediary and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account & All Data.
  4. Confirm. Your account and every record are deleted immediately and permanently — this cannot be undone.

Option 2 — request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email us from the address associated with your account (the one you used with Apple or Google sign-in) and we will delete your account and all associated data.

Email help@artifactai.co.nz

We may ask you to confirm details only you would know (such as the approximate date you created your account) before completing a request made this way, to avoid deleting the wrong account.

What gets deleted

Both options permanently remove:

  • Your account and sign-in identity
  • Every injection and injection record
  • Stock, batch, and stock history data
  • Symptoms, weight records, and notes
  • Appointments, providers, and provider questions
  • Reminders and purchase/entitlement records

Deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed. We do not retain a backup copy of deleted health records after deletion completes.
